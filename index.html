What is an Oopbuy spreadsheet?

In product research, an Oopbuy spreadsheet is a sheet for organizing listing links, selected variants, measurements, price notes, and questions still to resolve. Its value comes from accurate records: a row in a sheet is a reference, not a guarantee of stock, authenticity, or quality.

02 / A useful decision sequence

Keep. Clarify.
Or move on.

This oopbuy spreadsheet guide starts with the questions that can change a decision. A shorter list of well-understood items is easier to compare than a long collection of uncertain links.

  1. 01

    Can you identify the exact option?

    A listing may contain several colors, sizes, or item types. Save the chosen option beside the URL. When several rows point to the same listing, give each variant its own clear label.

    If unclear → mark “variant unconfirmed.”
  2. 02

    Do the measurements answer your fit question?

    Keep units visible. Distinguish flat garment width from body circumference, and item length from model height. For sets, check both pieces; for pants, separate waist, rise, and inseam.

    If incomplete → ask for the missing measurement.
  3. 03

    Is the evidence tied to that item?

    Separate catalog imagery, seller photos, and inspection photos. Note which selected variant a photo shows. Images can reveal visible details, but cannot by themselves establish authenticity or hidden material properties.

    If uncertain → record the limitation explicitly.
  4. 04

    Can you compare costs on the same basis?

    Record item price and currency, then list delivery, service fees, international shipping, and applicable taxes separately. Use the same currency and exchange-rate date when comparing totals.

    If charges are unknown → keep the total provisional.

06 / Direct answers

Before you
follow a link.

What is an Oopbuy spreadsheet used for?

In product research, an Oopbuy spreadsheet is used to keep listing links, selected variants, measurements, price notes, and unanswered questions together. A spreadsheet entry is a reference, not proof of stock, authenticity, or product quality.

Does this page provide a live spreadsheet or official Oopbuy service?

No. This is an independent research guide and CAFANS category directory. It does not provide a live product spreadsheet, an Oopbuy account, checkout, order management, or official platform support.

What should I do when a product link stops working?

Mark the entry as unavailable or unverified and record the date checked. Keep its descriptive notes for reference, but verify any replacement listing independently because its seller, variant, measurements, and price may differ.

How should I compare clothing and sneaker sizes?

For clothing, record the measurement method and compare the listing measurements with a garment that fits you. For sneakers, record the sizing system and consult the specific model's size information. Size labels alone are not enough for a reliable comparison.

Can brand photos prove what a seller will deliver?

No. Editorial brand photos illustrate design references. They do not establish a seller's inventory, authorization, authenticity, materials, or the condition of an individual item.

Which costs belong in a product comparison?

Record the item price, currency, domestic delivery, service or payment fees, international shipping, and applicable taxes or duties. Keep unknown charges separate from confirmed amounts instead of treating them as zero.
